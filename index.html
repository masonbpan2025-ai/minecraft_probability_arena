NEU (Net Expected Utility)
Resource Utility Conversion
To optimize choices mathematically, diverse resources must convert into a common "currency" (Utility). Since MP is rare and recovers slowly, spending 10 MP (20% of your max) is penalized heavily as -15 Utility Value. Contrastingly, Sword Durability is plentiful, costing only -2 Utility Value.

Example 1: Fireball vs Spider
• Spider is weak to Fireball (2.0x Dmg)
• Expected Dmg = 1.0 prob × 30 dmg × 2.0 weak = 60
• Resource Cost = 10 MP = -15 Utility
NEU = 60 - 15 = 45.0 (Excellent)

Example 2: Fireball vs Enderman
• Enderman resists Fireball (0.5x Dmg) & has a 30% dodge.
• Expected Dmg = 0.7 prob × 30 dmg × 0.5 resist = 10.5
• Resource Cost = 10 MP = -15 Utility
NEU = 10.5 - 15 = -4.5 (Terrible, do not waste MP)
= (Prob × Dmg × Element) - Resource Cost
